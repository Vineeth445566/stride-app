# Privacy Policy — Stride Step Counter

**Last updated:** October 9, 2026

Stride Step Counter ("Stride", "we", "our") is a free pedometer app.
This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data we do NOT collect

- **No account, no login.** You can use every feature without signing up.
- **No personal identifiers.** We do not ask for or store your name, email,
  or phone number.
- **No location.** Stride counts steps with your phone's built-in step-counter
  sensor. It does not use GPS and never records where you walk.
- **No health-data upload.** Your steps, distance, calories, height and weight
  are stored **only on your device** (in the app's private storage). They are
  never transmitted to us or to any third-party server.

## Advertising

Stride is supported by ads served through **Google AdMob**. To show ads, the
app requires internet access. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the
advertising ID) and use them to serve and measure ads, including personalized
ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android system settings
(Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Permissions the app requests

- **Physical activity recognition** (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`) — required to
  count your steps with the step-counter sensor.
- **Internet / network state** — required only to load ads. Step counting
  works fully offline.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all of your data lives on your device, deleting the app permanently
deletes it. There is nothing stored on our servers to request or delete.

## Children

Stride does not knowingly collect data from children. The app contains
advertising; ad content is subject to Google AdMob's policies.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL
with a new "Last updated" date.
