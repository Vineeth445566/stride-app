## Full description
Looking for a simple, accurate step counter? Stride is a free pedometer that
automatically tracks your daily steps, calories, distance and active time —
right on your phone.

KEY FEATURES

🚶 Automatic step tracking
Just open the app and walk. Stride uses your phone's built-in step sensor to
count steps whether the phone is in your hand, pocket or bag — even with the
screen locked. No wearable needed.

🔋 Offline & battery friendly
No GPS tracking, so it sips battery. Step counting works fully offline; only
the ads need an internet connection.

🎯 Daily goal ring
Set your own daily target (10,000 steps by default) and watch the progress
ring fill as you move through the day.

⏸️ Pause & resume
Pause tracking while driving and resume whenever you like — paused time is
never counted.

📊 Charts by day, week, month & year
See your trends at a glance: hourly bars for today, plus weekly, monthly and
yearly history with totals and daily averages.

🎨 Unlockable themes
Earn extra color themes by watching a short optional ad — no purchase needed.

🔐 Private by design
No account, no sign-up, no location tracking. Your data never leaves your
phone.

PLEASE NOTE
• For accurate distance and calorie estimates, enter your real height and
  weight in Settings.
• Step counting uses the phone's hardware step-counter sensor; a small number
  of very old devices may not have one.

Walk more, know more. Download Stride today.
